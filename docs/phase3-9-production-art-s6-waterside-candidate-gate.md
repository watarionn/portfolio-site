# Phase 3.9 - Waterside S6 Candidate Gate

Updated: 2026-09-30

Status: **mandatory pre-generation / pre-acceptance gate**

This checklist converts the active Portfolio Storybook City Style Bible into explicit Waterside Play candidate rules.

A candidate that fails any hard gate is rejected before production acceptance, even if parts of the image are visually attractive.

## Hard source-preservation gates

- [ ] Base candidate starts from the accepted Waterside S4 scene, not from a regenerated replacement scene.
- [ ] Production Locked Aquarium remains at the accepted left-side placement.
- [ ] Aquarium final pixels are restored exactly before acceptance if an editing workflow touches them.
- [ ] Central canal remains in the same broad composition.
- [ ] Stone bridge remains readable and unblocked.
- [ ] Distant town and aqueduct remain part of the established depth structure.
- [ ] Existing daylight direction and broad color grade remain compatible.
- [ ] No extra project building is invented beyond Aquarium, HoloCa and Word Generator.

## Bible style gates

- [ ] Image reads as illustrated / handcrafted, not game-engine rendered.
- [ ] Base palette remains low-to-mid saturation.
- [ ] Warm natural neutrals dominate.
- [ ] Strong accents stay localized.
- [ ] Controlled irregularity appears in roofs, facade width, eaves, windows and terrain integration.
- [ ] Important buildings are recognizable by silhouette before detail.
- [ ] Distant scenery has lower contrast, saturation, edge sharpness and micro-detail.
- [ ] Detail is concentrated around current project architecture and immediate foreground.
- [ ] No uniform black outline treatment.
- [ ] No photorealistic / PBR reflections.
- [ ] No generated text is required for building identity.

## District-composition gates

- [ ] Aquarium is the dominant / hero mass.
- [ ] HoloCa is a medium-scale angled midground mass.
- [ ] Word Generator is smaller, deeper and partly occluded.
- [ ] The three project buildings are not equal in size.
- [ ] The three project buildings are not arranged as front-facing cards.
- [ ] Water remains visual breathing space.
- [ ] Some route or depth continuation remains partially hidden.
- [ ] Right-side terrain remains explorable rather than completely filled.
- [ ] Project silhouettes remain suitable for semantic hotspot registration.

## HoloCa identity

Preferred architectural identity:

- angled card shop integrated into the right-side terrace
- compact asymmetric roof silhouette
- readable entrance
- weathered red / muted blue-green accent
- one restrained card-related geometric motif
- no readable generated title required

Reject if:

- it reads primarily as a workshop
- it is a floating storefront cutout
- it competes with Aquarium in mass or detail

## Word Generator identity

Preferred architectural identity:

- smaller upper-right curiosity shop
- partly hidden by tree / wall / elevation
- warm ochre / muted brick / dusty green accent
- one restrained tabletop-game / word-play architectural motif
- distinct silhouette from HoloCa
- no readable generated title required

Reject if:

- it is another copy of the HoloCa timber shop
- its motif requires text to understand
- it becomes equal in scale to the midground building

## Edit-region rule for C2+

Normal editable region:

- approximately x = 60-94% of scene width
- approximately y = 20-68% of scene height

Protected region:

- Aquarium and immediate locked placement area
- left waterfront
- broad central water body
- central bridge
- major distant skyline

Minor blending across the edit boundary is allowed only when needed for terrain integration.

The final accepted Aquarium must still be restored from the exact Production Locked source.
