# EXPLORE Forest Area B Art Brief

Status: **PHASE 2-E COMPOSITION BRIEF**
Date: 2026-09-23

## Role

Area B follows the fully locked Area A. It must feel like the same illustrated forest world, while becoming slightly deeper and more winding. Area B is not a landmark district and must remain visually quieter than later special areas E/F/G.

## Live desktop interaction pockets

Preserve these canonical positions as composition constraints:

- nozoki: x14 y27
- kottoi: x70 y26
- kyobate: x10 y61
- itab: x71 y63
- kugunari: x39 y18
- senpoji: x42 y69
- pashikuru: x84 y45

Each pocket should retain roughly 14-18% canvas width and 12-16% height of low-detail ground/foliage behind the HTML/CSS sign and possible expanded quiz.

## Navigation corridors

- left exit -> Area A, left-center
- right exit -> Area D, right-center
- bottom exit -> Area C, bottom-center
- keep the top-right region calm enough for the discovery notebook

## Composition

Use Area A as the continuity baseline: hand-drawn contour, simplified painterly surfaces, muted yellow-green / olive / beige / brown, gentle storybook mystery atmosphere. Do not copy a specific game scene.

Differentiate B with a slightly narrower, more serpentine path and denser middle-distance tree rhythm. Keep the lower center readable for the route to Area C. Avoid large props, buildings, ruins, rails, signs, lanterns, fantasy effects, dramatic fog, or horror cues.

Suggested tree mass:
- upper-left: medium, leaving x14/y27 readable
- upper-center: light around x39/y18
- upper-right: medium, leaving x70/y26 readable
- mid-right: restrained around x84/y45 and right exit
- lower-left: trunk mass must not invade x10/y61
- lower-center/right: calm ground for x42/y69 and x71/y63

## Output masters

Desktop: target 1536x1024, composition designed around the seven canonical desktop pockets.

Mobile: produce a separate portrait companion master after desktop adoption. Do not center-crop the desktop art. Recompose the same forest motifs around a dedicated mobile coordinate set, following the Area A responsive precedent.

## Hard exclusions

No baked-in place names, quiz text, arrows, notebook, buttons, icons, or navigation UI. No people, houses, rail tracks, photorealism, generic fantasy glow, or high-detail decoration under interaction pockets.

## Acceptance gate

1. Same-world continuity with Area A.
2. Seven desktop pockets remain readable.
3. Three exit corridors remain usable.
4. Top-right notebook safe zone remains calm.
5. B has a subtly deeper/winding identity without becoming a special landmark area.
6. Mobile recomposition remains feasible.
7. All interactive labels remain HTML/CSS, never baked into the art.
