# Phase 3.9 - Hybrid World Composition Blueprint

Updated: 2026-09-18
Status: composition direction

## Core decision

Use a hybrid composition. T1 Wide Atlas supplies the scale of the master world. T2 Balanced Storybook supplies the scale of the initial visitor camera.

Working principle: **The world is large. The initially visible Portfolio City is small and readable.**

The generated comparison sheet is reference evidence only. Its terrain, architecture, labels, and exact district positions are not production-approved.

## Three nested bounds

1. **Master World Bound**: the complete authored world-space. It is landscape-biased and substantially larger than the launch view. Exact logical rows and columns remain undecided.
2. **Published World Bound**: geography currently justified by published districts and their connections. It expands when a district is formally added.
3. **Initial Camera Bound**: the entry view containing the four current districts, connecting geography, clouded edges, and selected glimpses beyond them. It does not show the entire master world.

## Reveal model

Use four reveal levels:

- R0 Published district: clear, interactive, labels or landmarks allowed.
- R1 Published connective geography: clear or nearly clear.
- R2 Frontier glimpse: partially clouded, terrain visible beneath, no unpublished district label.
- R3 Deep unrevealed world: strongly obscured or outside the initial camera; no launch interaction required.

Cloud is an organic world-space frontier belt, not a rectangular screen mask. It can follow geography and leave small windows onto distant terrain.

## Expansion fronts

The launch composition preserves at least three plausible future district fronts without deciding their themes:

- F-A: land route such as a road, valley, plain, or forest corridor.
- F-B: a second land route with contrasting terrain character.
- F-C: a water, coast, bridge, or other special geographic route.

At least two fronts should appear as R2 glimpses in the initial composition. The third may remain in R3.

## District 5 and 6 simulation

District 5 opens one reserved front. Its connection becomes R1, its district footprint becomes R0, and cloud moves locally beyond it. Existing district homes remain fixed.

District 6 should preferably open a different front. This proves multidirectional expansion instead of a single growth corridor.

## Current district continuity

Preserve thematic memory without locking geometry:

- Observatory remains elevated or observational.
- Archive remains knowledge and search oriented.
- Workshop remains constructive and active.
- Waterside retains a meaningful water relationship.

The World Map may offset or stagger these regions when geography benefits.

## Central Commons

Do not force Central Commons into a fifth district. Keep its role flexible: World Map landmark, connective civic space, or shared location represented within District Views. Choose later based on navigation value.

## Production scope

A large conceptual world does not require final-detail painting everywhere at launch.

- W0 published core: final World Map quality.
- W0 frontier belt: enough quality for cloud glimpses.
- deep reserve: blockout, low-detail authored geography, or deferred tiles.
- future expansion: promoted to final quality when published.

## Camera behavior

Desktop frames all four current districts plus meaningful R2 glimpses and may allow limited pan inside the published-plus-frontier envelope. It must not expose unfinished deep reserve.

Mobile may crop the peripheral belt more tightly while keeping current districts discoverable. Edge clouds communicate continuation. The master world is shared; camera constraints differ by viewport.

## Composition-study result

The abstract comparison established that a wide master world communicates scale better than a map tightly wrapped around four districts, but showing the entire world at launch makes the current portfolio too small. A balanced camera crop inside a wide world supplies both readability and anticipation.

## Gate

Hybrid World Composition Blueprint: **COMPLETE**

Carry forward: landscape-biased large Master World; compact Initial Camera; separate Published World Bound; organic cloud frontier; R0-R3 reveal levels; at least three expansion fronts; different fronts preferred for District 5 and 6; flexible Central Commons role; deep world may remain low-detail or deferred.

Still unlocked: exact master aspect ratio, logical grid count, landmass shape, district coordinates, expansion-front directions, and cloud artwork.
