# CP-D2 Published Cluster Composition Test

Updated: 2026-09-19
Status: PASS as non-final composition blockout

## Purpose
Test the locked 16x12 address topology in visitor view without exposing the grid or producing final art.

## Result
- W0 homes remain H05 Observatory / G06 Archive / I06 Workshop / H07 Waterside.
- Visitor view hides all cell boundaries.
- Mainland silhouette crosses logical cells and is intentionally asymmetric.
- Observatory reads against an elevated northern ridge.
- Waterside meets a southern inlet rather than merely sitting below the other districts.
- Archive and Workshop remain land-connected.
- Central Commons is reduced to irregular connective seam space rather than a fifth focal district.
- F-A Inland, F-B Coastal, and F-C Offshore are visually distinguishable reveal directions.
- Offshore islands are visible as world-continuation evidence without making current districts insular.
- Desktop can show the W0 cluster plus frontier context.
- Mobile preserves district scale and intentionally crops the world for short-pan navigation.

## Four-quadrant audit
PASS. The composition avoids equal quadrants by using:
1. offset district focal points,
2. irregular mainland silhouette,
3. diagonal/curved connective routes,
4. non-central Commons geometry,
5. south inlet and east offshore interruption,
6. asymmetric frontier veil.
