## Why this registration exists

The North Crown watercolor experiment achieved the desired muted storybook mood, but geometry QA showed that its west/east shoulder terraces and water/cliff placement do not reliably preserve the fixed Sphere / HoloScope / Prime Dot Art and reserve coordinates.

Therefore the watercolor image is retained as **style reference only**. It is not the geometry authority.

## Geometry authority

North Crown geometry is now explicitly registered in:

- `docs/phase3-6-r0-north-registration.json`
- `docs/phase3-6-r0-master-plate-blockout.json`

The registered master bounds are `x=0..3072`, `y=-768..768`.

### H3

The northern reserve crown is the only H3 mass. It contains both Observatory reserve parcels and keeps the north expansion corridor open.

### H2

Three separate H2 shoulders are locked for:

- Sphere west terrace
- HoloScope central terrace
- Prime Dot Art east terrace

The H2 shapes may receive watercolor-softened edges, but their buildable interior areas may not be moved into water, hard cliff or inaccessible slopes.

## Protected open areas

The rough terrain must preserve five dry clearings:

- OBS-R1 `(1320,-220)`
- OBS-R2 `(1860,-220)`
- Sphere `(980,600)`
- HoloScope `(1600,360)`
- Prime Dot Art `(2220,600)`

These are not decorative ellipses in final art. They are protection envelopes only. Final watercolor should read as ordinary open ground.

## Routes

The exact rough-stage routes are:

- North spine: EC-N → H3 → H2 → G-NORTH
- West access: central spine → Sphere
- East access: central spine → Prime Dot Art

The north axis must never be blocked by a cliff, water body or landmark.

## Art rule

The style reference remains valuable for:

- muted watercolor palette
- soft hand-inked cliff contours
- grouped vegetation
- aged-paper warmth
- low micro-detail

It may **not** be used to infer geography.
